Global Warming

The Earth is getting a fever.

What is it?

The Blanket Effect

Pollution acts like a heavy blanket around the Earth. It traps heat and makes the world warmer every year.

The Cause

We burn coal, oil, and gas for energy. We also cut down too many trees. These activities create "greenhouse gases.

Ice is Melting

The poles are melting. Polar bears are losing their homes.

Many cities near the ocean might be underwater in the future.

Drinkable Water

Most of Earth is covered in water, but it's salty. Only 1% is fresh water available for us to drink.

How we waste it

Leaking Taps

One dripping tap can waste 20 gallons of water a day.

Long Showers

Taking 20-minute showers uses massive amounts of energy and water.

Take Action Today
